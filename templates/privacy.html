Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At CSGOTrading, we take your privacy and data security seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, and protect your personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and relevant online safety principles.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use our services, we collect:

  • Username
  • Email address
  • Account password

We do not collect unnecessary personal information or sensitive personal data.

2. Data Storage & Security

Passwords

Your password is never stored in plain text. All passwords are securely hashed using industry-standard cryptographic hashing algorithms.

  • Passwords cannot be viewed or retrieved by staff
  • Passwords cannot be reversed
  • Only secure verification is possible

Email Addresses

Email addresses are stored in an encrypted format to prevent unauthorised access and data exposure.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Account authentication and security
  • Enabling messaging and trading functionality
  • Providing essential platform services

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under UK GDPR, data is processed based on:

  • User consent
  • Service necessity
  • Legitimate interest in platform security

5. Data Retention

Personal data is retained only while your account remains active or as required for legal and security purposes. Account deletion may be requested where applicable.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request corrections
  • Request account and data deletion
  • Restrict or object to certain processing

7. Online Safety

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect user data, including restricted access controls and secure authentication practices.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect legal or technical changes. Any updates will be published on this page.

If you have questions regarding this policy, please contact the site administrator.
